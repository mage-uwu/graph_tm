Tiny BitNet b1.58 byte-level language models from stage_B/bitnet/bitnet.c (format BITNET02: header with the
architecture, then float32 latent weights; the ternary / int8 model is derived on load).

Training: --task text --data wiki_11m.txt --steps 3000 --eval-every 500 --eval-batches 8 (defaults otherwise: d 128,
2 layers, 4 heads, hidden 512, seq 64, batch 32, lr 3e-3, seed 1). wiki_11m.txt = the first 10,102,653 bytes of
WikiText-103 raw train text (streamed, paragraphs one per line, headings dropped) followed by the whole WikiText-103
validation text (1,122,517 bytes), so bitnet's 90/10 split puts exactly the validation text in the validation part.
3000 steps x 2048 bytes = 6.1M training bytes, no repeats.

Validation = every 64-byte window of the WikiText-103 validation text (bitnet eval), nats per byte:
